Privacy Policy for InHand

Last Updated: March 22, 2026

Privacy Commitment: InHand (Smart Pantry Scan) is designed to be a local-first application. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

1. Data Collection & Storage

All inventory data, including item names and expiration dates, is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a central database of your pantry contents.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide product information, the app sends barcode data to the Open Food Facts API. This data is used solely to retrieve nutritional and product information. No personal identifiers are attached to these requests.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: Used exclusively for barcode scanning. Images are processed locally and are not uploaded to our servers.

4. Contact

For questions, visit the project repository: GitHub - Smart Pantry Scan
